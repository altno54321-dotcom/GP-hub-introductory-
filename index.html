Install GP Hub on Your Device

GP Hub works like a native app. Add it to your phone or desktop home screen for instant access.

🍎

For iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open GP Hub in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner. Done!
🤖

For Android & Desktop (Chrome)

  1. Open GP Hub in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm the prompt. GP Hub is now installed!
